Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information you enter

The certificate details you type (parties, goods, signature) are processed in your own browser to create the PDF. They are not sent to or stored on this website's servers. Party details may be saved in your browser's local storage so you do not need to re-enter them; you can clear this by clearing your browser data.

Bill of materials analysis

The free origin checker runs entirely in your browser — your BOM is not sent anywhere. If you use the optional "AI check", your product description, HTS code, values and bill of materials are sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service (Anthropic) only to produce the origin analysis. This website does not keep a copy. Do not upload information you are not permitted to share.

To limit the optional AI check to one per visitor, the service stores a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address for up to 30 days. The IP address itself is not stored and cannot be read back from the hash.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings. For more information see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
