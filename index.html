Common questions

Questions steel and auto parts companies often ask me.

Is USMCA the same as CUSMA?

Yes. It is the same agreement. Canada calls it CUSMA (Canada–United States–Mexico Agreement), the United States calls it USMCA, and Mexico calls it T-MEC.

Do my auto parts automatically qualify for USMCA / CUSMA because they are made in Canada?

No. Goods qualify only if they meet the product-specific rule of origin for their tariff classification — usually a tariff shift, a regional value content requirement, or both. Automotive goods also have their own additional rules. Each part should be analyzed and documented.

Can you help with Section 232 tariffs on steel and auto parts?

Yes. I review which entries are affected, how the duty is applied, and where sourcing, routing or documentation choices change the exposure.

What is Finance Canada remission?

Remission is a Department of Finance Canada process that can relieve Canadian surtaxes in specific situations, such as when the goods cannot be sourced domestically or are required under existing contracts. A strong application depends on clear evidence.
